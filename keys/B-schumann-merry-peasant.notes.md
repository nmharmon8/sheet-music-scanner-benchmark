**Layout.** 5 systems, 21 measures. M1 is a one-eighth pickup. Systems hold M1–M5, M6–M9, M10–M13, M14–M17 and
M18–M21. There is a thin double barline after M9 and a final barline after M21. Key F major (-1), C (4/4). The
key has 118 fingers.

**Printed but not in the key**
- Title in Italian, French and English, the number "10.", the page number 11, plate "E.R. 190" and signature "z".
- Allegramente ♩=112 (above M1); *ben cantando il basso* (under the LH, M1–M2).
- *(poco rit.)* above M11 and M17. *(a tempo)* above the last eighth of M11 and M17, running into M12 and M18.
- The brackets around *(mf)* in M9 and M15. They are written as plain `\mf`.
- Pedal: Ricordi's bracket marks under the bass, one per half bar (beats 1–2 and beats 3–4), in M2–M10, M12–M16
  and M18–M21. M11 and M17 have only the beats 3–4 bracket. M1 has none.

**Readings to note**
- Finger substitutions: the "45" with an arc on RH G4 (M10, M16) is `4-5`. The "21" with an arc on LH G3 (M15,
  M21) is `2-1`.
- One finger printed above a chord goes on its top note (`[. . 5]`). Two stacked fingers go on the two notes,
  bottom to top: "3 over 2" is `[2 3]` (M15, M21) and "4 over 1" is `[1 4]` (M21).
- Courtesy accidentals are written as printed. M7 has a flat on RH B4 and a bracketed (♭) on LH B3, and M10 has a
  flat on RH B4. The natural on B in M5, M9, M11 and M17 is also printed.
- **Uncertain, M3 and M7, RH second chord.** Both bars print only two noteheads, Bb4 and D5, with the staccato dot
  sitting low in the F4 space. I read the dot as staccato rather than an F4 notehead, because it is the size of the
  other staccato dots and the same in both bars. Repeating the first chord, [F4 Bb4 D5], would be the other
  reading.
- M13 and M19, RH beats 2 and 4: the B4 notehead hangs from the down-stem. So the RH line has D5 alone and the
  RH2 line has [F4 B4].
- M15 and M21, LH: there are two voices, stems up and down. The F3 on beat 3 has both stems, so it is written in
  both voices (a unison; the copy is ignored). Voice 2 is a spacer on beat 4.
- Dynamics printed between the staves (f in M1 and M5, (mf) in M9 and M15) are on the LH line, next to the LH
  melody's upbeat. M11 and M17 each print two f's, one above the RH and one below the LH, and both are written.

**Reference edition:** none. The catalog has no Op. 68 No. 10, and the key was read from the image alone.
